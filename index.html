Welcome to KES School Charsadda

At KES School Charsadda (King Edward’s School), we aim to provide an inspiring educational environment that develops confident, responsible, and successful students. Our curriculum blends modern teaching methods with traditional values.

We are dedicated to nurturing each child's intellectual, physical, and emotional growth to prepare them for global opportunities.

Our Vision

To be a premier institution recognized for academic distinction and character development.

Our Mission

To foster a creative and supportive learning atmosphere that encourages critical thinking and Islamic morals.

Academic Programs
